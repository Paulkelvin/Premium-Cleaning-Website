Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026.

Information we collect

We collect the information you submit through our quote, booking, contact, and payment forms, including your name, email, phone number, service address, service details, preferred contact method, and messages. When you use our website we also receive basic technical information, such as the pages you visit and your browser and device type.

How we use information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, prepare estimates, schedule and provide services, send confirmations and payment links, provide customer support, and improve our website and business operations. We contact you about your request using the contact details you provide.

Payments

Online payments are processed by Square. Card details are entered on Square's secure checkout and are not stored by RS Cleaning Collective. We receive payment confirmations, such as the amount, date, and status of a payment.

Service providers

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, including Supabase (secure storage of form submissions and bookings), Square (payments), Google (business email), and Cloudflare (website hosting and security). These providers may only use the information to provide their services to us.

Website analytics and cookies

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits; it does not use cookies. We may also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website and which pages lead to quote requests. Google Analytics uses cookies; you can block cookies in your browser settings or use Google's opt-out tool at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Our quote tool may also save your in-progress quote in your browser so you don't lose it if you leave the page.

Data storage and security

Form submissions and bookings are stored in a secure database with access limited to authorized RS Cleaning Collective staff. We keep records for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet business, tax, and legal requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by emailing or calling us. Our website is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to ryann@rslegalcollective.com or 240-823-4466.
