About us

Cleaning service with polish, structure, and genuine care.

PristinePro Cleaning helps busy households, property managers, and local teams keep their spaces calm, healthy, and ready for life.

We built the company around trust and detail.

Premium cleaning is not just a sparkling sink. It is arriving when promised, understanding the priorities, communicating clearly, and leaving the space better than expected.

Our team focuses on practical hospitality: we respect your time, your property, your preferences, and the details that make a service feel truly professional.

  • Clear scopes before work begins.
  • Professional supplies and equipment available.
  • Recurring and one-time cleaning options.
  • Respectful service for homes, rentals, offices, and move-outs.
