FAQs

Answers before you request a quote or book.

Here are the questions clients usually ask before their first cleaning service.

How does pricing work?

Pricing is based on the service type, size of the property, number of rooms, current condition, cleaning frequency, and any add-ons.

Are quotes final?

Online quotes are starting estimates. We confirm the final scope and price after reviewing your details and any special conditions.

How does booking work?

Submit a quote or appointment request, then our team confirms availability, access instructions, arrival window, and service scope.

How does payment work?

Payment details are confirmed before service. Commercial and recurring clients may qualify for invoice-based billing.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Contact us as early as possible. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee depending on the appointment type.

What is included in deep cleaning?

Deep cleaning includes standard cleaning tasks plus more detailed attention to buildup, baseboards, fixtures, cabinet fronts, detail dusting, and hard-to-reach areas.

What is included in standard cleaning?

Standard cleaning covers routine maintenance: kitchens, bathrooms, dusting, floors, trash, counters, sinks, mirrors, and high-touch surfaces.

Do you offer recurring cleaning?

Yes. Weekly, biweekly, and monthly service plans are available for homes and workplaces.

What should I expect from before and after results?

Results depend on the condition of the space, surface age, stains, and buildup. We set expectations before work begins.

Do you bring supplies and equipment?

Yes. We can bring professional supplies and equipment. We can also use your preferred products if requested in advance.

What areas do you cover?

We serve Austin, TX and nearby areas including Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Lakeway, Bee Cave, Georgetown, and Leander.
