Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Last updated May 30, 2026.

At RS Cleaning Collective, we understand that schedules change. To provide the best service to all of our clients, we kindly ask for the following notice regarding cancellations and rescheduling:

48-hour notice required

Clients may cancel or reschedule an appointment without penalty by providing at least 48 hours’ notice prior to the scheduled cleaning.

Late cancellations

Appointments canceled with less than 48 hours’ notice may be subject to a cancellation fee equal to 50% of the scheduled service cost.

Same-day cancellations

Appointments canceled on the day of service may be charged up to 100% of the scheduled service cost, as our team has reserved that time specifically for your home.

Lockouts & no-show appointments

If our team arrives and is unable to access the property, or if the appointment is missed without notice, the appointment will be considered a no-show and the full service fee may be charged.

Recurring service clients

Clients on weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly recurring schedules who repeatedly cancel or reschedule appointments may be removed from their reserved time slot.

Emergencies

We understand that unexpected situations arise. Cancellation fees may be waived at management’s discretion for emergencies, severe weather, illness, or other unforeseen circumstances.

By scheduling service with RS Cleaning Collective, you acknowledge and agree to this cancellation policy. See also our Terms of Service.

To cancel or reschedule, contact us as early as possible or call 202-262-6379.
